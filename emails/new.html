You entered the Fold.

It is the closest thing to a doorway the universe will offer you, and it does not behave like a door. Spacetime bends. Distance compresses. The body holds. The mind drifts.

Some passengers report colors that have no names. Some report hearing voices in languages they once dreamed in. Some report nothing at all and emerge changed in ways they cannot describe. The Fold is not weather you can prepare for. It is a passage that takes its passengers.

It also gives.

Every passenger receives a gift in passage. The colony calls it a dream gift — a fragment of where you are going, transmitted through the substrate of the Fold while your conscious mind is elsewhere. You receive it whether you remember the receiving or not. You will recognize it when you read it. It will already feel like something you knew.
