◈Orbital Three · “The Ears” · Open Channel

Nobody has answered
in a very long time.

And yet Lieutenant F. Muro keeps the channel open. Every watch. Just in case.

Humanity crossed the Fold to build its first colony beyond the solar system. Then the Fold closed behind them. No way home. No word from Earth. Just a severed relay and the dark.

Muro mans the comms station — alone — listening to static that no longer carries anyone. He talks to it anyway. He signs off to a man named George who cannot hear him. He tells the empty band about his day because there is no one else to tell.

He is not a hero. He is a tired man on a lonely post, doing the one small thing he still knows how to do: keep listening, and hope that someday the static answers back.

Entry 214. Signal integrity: nominal. At 0300 the hum started — not through the speakers, but up through the deck plating and into his back teeth before his ears caught it.

Something from the planet below, reaching through his own power grid like it was strung on a wire.
